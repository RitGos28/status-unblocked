14. The Status-Update Tax

Based on the Student Edition challenge: Team Task & Standup Bot

Scenario — You’re on a distributed team where daily standups and status-chasing eat everyone’s morning.

Solve this — A bot that collects short async updates from each member and summarises progress and blockers into one daily digest.

Data & free tools — Made-up daily updates; a bot on Teams / Slack / Discord, or a web form + spreadsheet.

Make it enterprise-grade:

• Make summaries faithful and link each blocker back to its source message.

• Write structured output back to a task tracker.

• Respect channel and privacy boundaries — don’t become surveillance.
